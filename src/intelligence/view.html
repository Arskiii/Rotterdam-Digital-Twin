04 / MODEL EVIDENCE

How close the model is to measured traffic

SIMULATION

The simulation is scored against NDW road sensors at three times of day, using weeks of archived weekday readings. One in five sensors is held out and never used to tune the comparison, so its error is an honest out-of-sample figure. The scores are published as they come out, good or bad.

Loading the published check…

Run your own signal comparison

Run actuated, coordinated and fixed signals from the same initial state and random seed. A stable 20% sample of NDW road sensors is held out for an independent flow-error check. One short run cannot establish forecasting accuracy.

05 / REUSE

Open, documented outputs

Read the same source catalog, modeled road exposure and aggregated CBS figures as JSON. The optional observation service has a current-feed endpoint and health check. Its record names follow SensorThings concepts; it is a documented subset rather than a claim of full OGC conformance.
